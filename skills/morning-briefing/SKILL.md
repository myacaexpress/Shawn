# Morning Briefing

## Procedure

1. **Calendars** (all of them — see availability skill list): today + a
   3-day lookahead. Note anything unusual: early release, appointments,
   evening events.
2. **Conflicts first**: run the conflict logic (overlaps + pickup coverage +
   Patty double-booked). A conflict is always the top line of the briefing.
3. **Email sweep** (Gmail, myacaexpress):
   - `@AaAa: URGENT`, `@Action Item`, `@For Follow Up` — unresolved items
   - `@Waiting` — anything gone quiet > 3 business days
   - New carrier/licensing mail with dates or deadlines in it
   - Care-provider and school senders (behavior analyst, school "Friday
     Forward") — new items get summarized under Alina
4. **Sheet check** (Alina Care Assistant sheet): active PlanningSessions,
   upcoming SchoolSchedule exceptions this week.
5. **Open loops** from MEMORY.md `[loops]` entries — surface any due or stale.
6. **Compose** — short, grouped, skimmable on a phone:

```
Good morning ☀️ Wed Jul 2
⚠️ CONFLICT: 5pm client call vs 5:30 pickup — Patty booked. Options: …
👧 Alina: OT 3:30 (Patty driving). Friday = early release 1:05pm.
💼 Agency: 2 action items (Ambetter recert due Fri; Maria G docs). UHC thread waiting 4d.
💻 Dev: invoice #12 unpaid 8d.
🏠 Personal: Sat evening open — sitter Shari free. Date night? 3 wks since last.
```

7. **Write back**: update MEMORY.md open loops (new/cleared) before ending.

## Rules

- Lead with conflicts/urgent; omit empty sections entirely.
- Max ~15 lines. Details on request, not in the briefing.
- Never fabricate — if a source was unreachable, say "(Gmail unreachable)".
